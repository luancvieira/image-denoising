Gray values of each sample share one window (0.1–99.9 percentiles of the fast, 8 min and 60 min central views). Each case (without and with aluminium standardization) uses the volumes and models of that case. Restored volumes use the epoch selected for each test plug in the leave-one-plug-out evaluation; direct transfer is the pretrained Restormer applied without training, and is not segmented. PSNR, SSIM, porosity φ and Dice are 3D values over the cropped volume; PSNR and SSIM are computed with respect to the 60 min reference, Dice with respect to its manual segmentation. Error maps show volume minus reference, clipped at ±2500 gray levels. Segmentations follow the article: the threshold of each pipeline is computed on the whole volume, and pore clusters smaller than 27 voxels (6-connectivity) are removed. The micro-CT volumes are 16-bit; the images shown here are 8-bit PNG renderings of each sample's gray window, at full resolution (one pixel per voxel) and with the origin at the lower left, as in the paper figures. Click an image to enlarge it; use the arrow keys to move between images or samples.
